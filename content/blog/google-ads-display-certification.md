When I left my job at Google with no marketing experience, "I'll learn
quickly" was mostly a promise to myself. This week I have the first
piece of actual proof: I passed the **Google Ads Display Certification**
through Google Skillshop.

## Why this one, and why first

Display was the logical starting point — I started by asking Claude
what a realistic plan looked like for starting my marketing business,
and the first thing it pointed me to was this certificate.

## What it does and doesn't prove

A certification isn't the same as running a real campaign with real
budget on the line. What it does prove: I can sit down with an
unfamiliar system, learn its logic, and pass a real exam on it with
zero background. That's the skill this whole business depends on
repeating, over and over, in channels I haven't touched yet.

## What's next

I'm going to complete the Google Analytics GA4 Certificate next.
