I left my software engineering job at Google to build my own business,
though I initially had no set direction.

Early on, I considered physical options like purchasing a laundromat.
However, after reflecting on my personal goals — specifically my desire
to move to New York City and explore a new lifestyle — I realized a
physical location would only tie me down, alongside the houses I needed
to sell.

I pivoted toward an entirely online model, which led me to digital
marketing. Although I had no prior experience in the field, I was
confident in my ability to learn quickly, and marketing offered the
perfect entry point into entrepreneurship.
