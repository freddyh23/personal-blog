Three weeks after going full-time on the business, I signed my first two
clients. Neither came from ads or a cold outreach campaign. Here's the
honest breakdown.

## The offer had to be embarrassingly specific

My first draft of the offer was vague — something like "I can help with
that." Useless. Nobody wakes up wanting vague help.

The version that worked was one specific sentence describing exactly what
I'd deliver, for a fixed fee, with one clear outcome.

Specific enough that the right person immediately knew if it fit.

## Where the clients actually came from

- **Client 1** — a former colleague who saw my [first post](/why-i-am-starting-a-marketing-consultancy)
  and forwarded it to a friend. Warm intro, closed in two calls.
- **Client 2** — someone who found an early version of this site by
  searching a very long, very specific phrase. That's the SEO bet paying
  off in miniature.

## What I'd tell my past self

1. Price the first project low enough to remove the risk, high enough to
   be taken seriously.
2. Write down what success looks like *before* you finish the work.
3. Publish. The second client existed because a page existed.
