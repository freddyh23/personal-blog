Before I touch a client's website, I'm treating my own as the lab. If I
can't move numbers here, I have no business charging for it elsewhere.

## The measurement stack

- **GA4** for behavior: which posts get read, how people move between
  pages, where they drop off.
- **Google Search Console** for search: which queries show my pages, my
  average position, and my click-through rate.

The two answer different questions. GA4 tells me what people do once they
arrive. Search Console tells me how they found me in the first place.

## The experiments I'm running

I'm keeping a running log of small, reversible tests:

1. **Meta descriptions** — rewriting them and watching click-through rate
   in Search Console.
2. **Internal linking** — every post links to at least two others to see
   if it lifts pages-per-session and indexing speed.
3. **Keyword targeting** — writing one post around a specific long-tail
   phrase real founders actually search.
4. **Title formats** — testing "how I…" narrative titles against plain
   "how to…" titles.

## Why this matters for clients

Every experiment here becomes a documented playbook I can reuse. When a
client asks "does this actually work?", I'll have my own before-and-after
screenshots instead of a shrug.
