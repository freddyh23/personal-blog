*Example entry — replace this with your own day-zero story.*

I spent the last several years working inside other people's companies.
Last month I handed in my notice to start my own thing — a business I'm
building from scratch, one decision at a time.

This site is where I'll document the whole journey — the wins, the
awkward moments, and the experiments that flop.

## Why now

A few reasons pushed me over the edge:

1. **I kept coming back to the same idea.** It wouldn't leave me alone, so
   it was time to actually try it instead of just thinking about it.
2. **I wanted to own my output.** Building in public means my work
   compounds instead of disappearing behind someone else's name.
3. **The best time to start was years ago. The next best time is now.**

## What "building in public" means here

Every post will include the actual decision, the reasoning, and — where I
can share it — the numbers. I'd rather be useful and specific than
polished and vague.

If you're doing something similar, [read the about page](/about) to see
what this site is about, or follow along as the [journal](/blog) grows.

## The first 30-day plan

- Figure out exactly what I'm building and who it's for
- Publish here regularly to keep myself honest
- Get real feedback from real people, as early as possible

More soon. This is day zero.
